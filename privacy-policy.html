Quick Alarm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Who operates Quick Alarm

Quick Alarm is operated by NgocPham. For privacy questions or requests, contact ngocpt192@gmail.com.

Data handled by the app

Quick Alarm stores your selected alarm interval, current alarm state and scheduled time, whether you have viewed the help screen, and a local app-open counter in private storage on your device. The counter helps determine when to show an interstitial ad and resets when an interstitial ad begins showing. The app does not create an account or send your alarm settings to the developer.

The app uses Android notification, alarm, and foreground-service features to schedule and sound alarms. It does not request your contacts, photos, or device location. The advertising SDK may infer a device's general location from its IP address, as described below.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Quick Alarm uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and interstitial ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share data with Google, including your IP address; app interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views; diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and device or account identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to signed-in accounts. This data is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location. Google states that data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Quick Alarm includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present applicable privacy choices. Where available, you can also manage or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's information about its data practices is available in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Alarm settings and help-screen state remain in the app's private storage until you clear Quick Alarm's app data or uninstall it. The local app-open counter resets when an interstitial ad begins showing and is otherwise stored with the app's data. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up or restore app data according to your device and backup settings. Quick Alarm does not operate its own server or keep a separate copy of your alarm settings. Google handles data collected by its advertising SDK under Google's own policies and retention practices. Quick Alarm does not provide accounts or a server-side account deletion function.

Children

Quick Alarm is a general-purpose alarm utility and is not designed specifically for children. The app's audience and advertising settings should be configured accurately in Google Play Console and AdMob.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the public policy address for Quick Alarm.
